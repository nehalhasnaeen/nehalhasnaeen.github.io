About Me

I build AI systems that see, decide and assist. My work spans multimodal computer vision, reinforcement learning and socially assistive robotics for Alzheimer’s and dementia care — moving from teleoperated robot platforms toward near-autonomous AI pipelines.

From 2023 to 2026 I was a Graduate Research Assistant at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, where I contributed to a field-deployable human–robot interaction platform for older adults, a two-agent reinforcement learning simulation for adaptive dementia care, and FujiView, a multimodal late-fusion model for scenic visibility prediction (IEEE/CVF WACV 2026).

Before that, at Idaho State University, I earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering (CGPA 3.96/4.00) working on physical-layer security for UHF RFID — using electromagnetic fingerprints for malware detection and digital twin identification. I hold a B.S. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET. I am currently applying to PhD programs.

RF security & signal processing · Idaho State University

UHF RFID EM Fingerprint-Based Digital Twin Web Resolver

2022–2023
IEEE RFID 2023 · Poster, IEEE DTPI 2022

Electromagnetic fingerprints as unclonable identifiers resolving physical RFID tags to cloud-hosted digital twins; 95–99% classification accuracy across tag types. HeroX Honorable Mention.

ML for Longitudinally Slotted Waveguide Characterisation

RFID Malware Detection via Physical-Layer EM Fingerprinting

2021–2022

Detects SQL-injection malware from RFID EM signatures without memory access; two novel spectral feature-reduction methods; 82.5% test accuracy.
